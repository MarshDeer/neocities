# What do you mean it's been over a year

Hey there

So... It's been a while huh?

I sorta stopped updating this place after December 2024. I can't (and don't 
want to) give too much personal information, but the gist of it is that I was 
in a less-than-ideal housing situation for about 11 months; pretty much until 
the start of November 2025, when we finally moved again.

2025 was fine in some ways, maybe even good in a couple, but it mostly just 
sucked and I wouldn't really want to do it again. I feel like most of my 
projects were frozen for the year and I've st kinda been in a limbo of starting 
projects and dropping them again.

Work was also really really really stressful between August and November. We got 
a massive project we were really unprepared for, and I worked around 1.7x my 
regular hours to just barely manage to finish the project (which the client 
still hasn't fucking paid us for. yay!). Naturally, this blew my disability the 
fuck up and I'm still sorta dealing with the fallout ^^;

Stuff's been getting better, though. Hence me managing to make
[xkcd 2501 gen](https://marshdeer.github.io/xkcd2501gen), update the 
[medialog](/medialog/index.html), hack an argentinian startup (writeup 
incoming) and, like, write this article to begin with. Things are finally 
looking up in a way that doesn't feel too real after a full year of stress, 
housing anxiety and near-constant dissociation :3

I don't really have much to say, really. Tumblr has gotten significantly worse 
since I last pretended I was gonna be able to write here with any degree of 
regularity, so I figured I'd drop by as a vague statement of commitment to 
write more. Deer's still alive, she was just surrounded by headlights for a 
while.
